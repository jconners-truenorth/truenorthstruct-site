About

True North Structural Engineering is led by Jacob Conners, a licensed Professional Engineer based in the Yampa Valley.

With over 15 years of engineering experience across structural and civil disciplines, the work is grounded in clarity, constructability, and long-term performance.

Jacob earned a Master’s degree in Structural Engineering from the University of Colorado Boulder and a Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering Technology from the Rochester Institute of Technology. His educational path combined advanced analytical training with practical, construction-oriented foundations.

After nearly a decade living and working in this valley, he established True North as an independent practice focused on residential structural design. The firm operates with an emphasis on technical rigor, clear documentation, and direct communication throughout the life of a project.

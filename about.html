About

True North Structural Engineering is led by Jacob Conners, a licensed Professional Engineer based in the Yampa Valley.

Jacob holds over 15 years of engineering experience across structural and civil disciplines. He earned a Master’s degree in Structural Engineering from the University of Colorado Boulder and a Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering Technology from the Rochester Institute of Technology.

His professional background includes high-end custom residences, large-scale remodels, foundation systems, and commercial renovations in mountain environments.

After nearly a decade living and working in this valley, he established True North as an independent practice built around clarity, technical rigor, and professional integrity.

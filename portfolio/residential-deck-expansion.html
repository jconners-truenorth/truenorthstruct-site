Residential Deck Expansion

Full structural design for an elevated deck expansion in Steamboat Springs, including existing framing evaluation, foundation upgrades, and coordinated stair details.

Expanding an existing elevated deck required more than simply extending the wood framing. True North evaluated the existing glulam beams and columns under the revised loading, allowing adequate existing members to remain while focusing new work where it was needed most.

The proposed expansion increased reactions at the four existing supports by approximately 14 to 15 percent. Because the existing small-diameter supports needed to be evaluated against current project requirements, the foundation design was updated to follow the geotechnical recommendation for new 12-inch-diameter drilled piers extending at least 20 feet deep.

The structural package included revised deck framing, foundation design, and stair and connection details for permit and construction. The work reflects a practical True North approach: reuse what performs, replace what does not, and document the result clearly.

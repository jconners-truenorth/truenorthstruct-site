Mountain Home

Gravity system design for a complex residential program in a high-elevation setting.

Photography: Brandon Huttenlocher

This mountain home required gravity framing design for a complex residential program in a high-elevation setting. The structure incorporates significant structural steel and heavy timber elements to support open interior volumes and architectural glazing features.

Site conditions introduced challenging foundation and retaining relationships, requiring careful coordination between building structure and adjacent site elements. Structural detailing addressed load path continuity and constructability within constrained conditions.

Framing strategies were developed to support vertical volume and expansive glazing while maintaining clean architectural lines and proportional balance.

Project details are presented in accordance with client confidentiality requirements.

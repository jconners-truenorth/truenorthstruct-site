Storm Meadows Townhome Expansions

Structural design and framing observations for extensive remodels and additions within an existing Steamboat Springs townhome development.

Project photography pending.

Storm Meadows involved extensive remodel and addition work across several connected townhomes. The work included structural framing observations for one phase of the project and full structural design for later remodel/addition work within the same development.

The design scope included expansions to lower deck areas, upper deck modifications, bedrooms added above existing carports, expanded upper-level living areas, and modest roof changes. The work required new structural design to fit within and around existing multifamily construction rather than starting from a clean building site.

Soda Creek Custom Compound

Full structural design for a custom mountain compound with connected living spaces, a detached caretaker residence, and custom recreational-space framing.

Project photography pending.

This project required full structural design for a custom mountain compound composed of connected living spaces and a separate caretaker residence. Structural systems included dimension lumber, engineered wood framing, LVL members, concrete, expressed steel beams, and exposed heavy timber rafters spanning large public rooms.

The design required resolving multiple roof diaphragms for lateral load paths, coordinating tight deflection limits around expansive glazing and large sliding door openings, and detailing long-span timber members at splice locations where gravity and lateral forces needed to transfer cleanly. Flat roof areas required careful evaluation of drifting and sliding snow loads.

A dedicated golf simulator room became an important coordination point. The room was located beneath a stair opening near a cantilevered condition, where conventional framing depth could have reduced usable swing clearance. The framing strategy was adjusted to preserve headroom while maintaining the structural load path, helping avoid a user-impacting obstruction in the finished space.

Several adjacent site retaining conditions required coordination with the building foundation systems. Project details are presented in accordance with assumed client confidentiality requirements.
